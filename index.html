    Kya yeh NCERT aur CBSE syllabus ke according hai?

    Haan. Har chapter NCERT ke section numbers ke saath chalta hai, aur chapters CBSE 2026-27 syllabus ke hisaab se hain. Textbook se cross-check karna aasaan hai.

    Kya yeh JEE aur NEET ke liye bhi kaam ki hai?

    Booklet ka main focus board exam aur concept clarity hai. Saath mein har chapter mein "JEE/NEET extra" boxes hain jo advanced concepts cover karte hain.

    Hinglish mein padh ke English exam kaise likhunga?

    Samjhaya Hinglish mein gaya hai, lekin har topic ke saath "Exam mein aise likho" box mein board ke liye poora English answer diya hai.

    Content dono mein same hai. Online book tum apni login ID se phone ya tablet par padhte ho. Paperback printed booklet hai jismein tum likh sakte ho, tick kar sakte ho aur colour kar sakte ho.

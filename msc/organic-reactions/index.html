What's inside: 7 topics

  1. Molecules kab react karte hain? Clayden pp. 107–110

    • Molecules hamesha move karte rehte hain
    • Har takkar se reaction nahi hota
    • Molecules ko paas kaun laata hai?
    • Reaction = electrons ka safar
    • Example 1: Cl- + H+ (charge attraction)
    • Example 2: NH3 + BH3 (koi charge nahi)

  2. Orbitals ka khel: HOMO aur LUMO Clayden pp. 110–112

    • Sahi direction mein takkar
    • MO diagram: bond banne se fayda kaise hota hai?
    • Energy jitni paas, fayda utna zyada
    • HOMO aur LUMO: kaunse do orbitals milenge?

  3. Nucleophile pehchano Clayden pp. 112–113

    • Nucleophile ke 4 types: lone pair, anion, π bond, σ bond
    • Cyanide: aam taur par C se attack, N se nahi
    • σ bond wala nucleophile: borohydride ka trap

  4. Electrophile pehchano Clayden pp. 113–116

    • Electrophile ke 4 types
    • H+: sabse simple electrophile
    • Khaali p orbital: BF3
    • Organic electrophiles: antibonding orbital + electronegative atom
    • C=O: π* LUMO
    • σ*: H–Cl aur CH3–Br
    • Br–Br: dipole zero, phir bhi strong electrophile

  5. Curly arrows: mechanism ki bhasha Clayden pp. 116–120

    • Ek curly arrow = do electrons
    • Do arrows: ek bond banata hai, ek todta hai
    • Arrow ka shape nahi, address important hai
    • Charge na banta hai, na mitta hai
    • π bond todo, σ bond ko chhedo mat
    • σ bond bhi nucleophile: BH4- ka poora mechanism
    • π bond bhi nucleophile: alkene + HBr

  6. Apna mechanism khud banao Clayden pp. 120–124

    • Do sawaal + Adeel Sir ka example 1: Me3N + CH3I
    • Paanch bonds wala carbon: sabse common galti
    • Multi-step mechanism: acetone + HCN
    • Adeel Sir ka example 3: ethanol → diethyl ether (H+ catalyst)
    • Step 2 mein lone pair O ya H par kyun nahi gaya?
    • Bilkul naya reaction? Phir bhi mechanism ban jaayega
    • Mechanism likhne ka 5-step formula

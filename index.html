      Questions and answers

      Does it follow the NCERT and CBSE syllabus?

      Yes. Every chapter follows the NCERT section numbers, and the chapters match the CBSE 2026-27 syllabus, so it is easy to cross-check with your textbook.

      Is it useful for JEE and NEET too?

      The main focus is the board exam and clear concepts. Every chapter also has "JEE/NEET extra" boxes covering advanced concepts.

      If I study in Hinglish, how will I write the exam in English?

      The explanations are in Hinglish, but every topic has an "Exam mein aise likho" box with the full board answer in English.

      What is the difference between Online and Paperback?

      The content is the same. You read the online book on your phone or tablet with your own login. The paperback is a printed booklet you can write in, tick and colour.

      How do I read the online book?

      After your order and payment, Adeel Sir sends you a login ID and password. Tap "Login" on this website, enter them, and read your chapters. One ID works on one device only, so don't share it. If you change your phone, contact Adeel Sir.

      How long can I read the online book?

      A bought online book is valid for 1 year (365 days) from the day you get your login. If a monthly plan is offered, you can also pay month by month and stop any time.

      How much does it cost?

      Each chapter's Online and Paperback price is shown under its cover. Pick chapters in "Build your pack" and the total adds up automatically. Delivery is charged extra for the paperback.

      How do I order?

      Choose a format and chapters in "Build your pack" above, pay by UPI right there, then tap "Send order on WhatsApp" with the payment screenshot. Your login ID and password come on WhatsApp.

      How do I pay?

      By UPI: scan the QR code on the order form, or tap "Pay with UPI app" on your phone (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app). For the paperback, Adeel Sir first tells you the delivery charge on WhatsApp, then you pay.

      Can I sign up without buying?

      Yes, it's free. Tap "Sign up free", tick your class, and the free sample of your class stays in your own library. When you buy chapters, they open on the same login ID.

      Can I see the book before buying?

      Yes. There is a free sample for both Class 11 and Class 12, with one complete topic from every chapter. Flip through it in "Free sample" above, or download the PDF.

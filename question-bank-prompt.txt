EXAMPLE
### 11-1
Q: 0.5 mol of CO_2 mein kitne molecules hain?
A) 6.022 × 10^{23}
B) 3.011 × 10^{23} *
C) 1.204 × 10^{24}
D) 3.011 × 10^{22}
Level: Board
Topic: Mole concept
Solution: Molecules = moles × N_A = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10^{23} = 3.011 × 10^{23}.
Option A 1 mol ka answer hai, 0.5 mol ka nahi.

Q: Assertion (A): 1 mol O_2 aur 1 mol N_2 mein molecules barabar hote hain. Reason (R): Ek mole mein hamesha N_A particles hote hain.
A) A aur R dono sahi, R sahi explanation hai *
B) A aur R dono sahi, par R sahi explanation nahi hai
C) A sahi, R galat
D) A galat, R sahi
Level: NEET
Topic: Mole concept
Solution: Har substance ke 1 mol mein N_A = 6.022 × 10^{23} particles hote hain, chahe mass alag ho. Isliye A sahi hai aur R hi uski wajah hai.
Option B tab hota jab R sahi hota par A ko explain na karta.

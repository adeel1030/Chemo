1Terms of use

  • Chemophile booklets are written and sold by Mohd Adeel Khan ("we", "Adeel Sir"). By ordering, or by using the website or the online reader, you agree to these policies.
  • Orders are placed on WhatsApp. An order is confirmed only after we receive the payment.
  • Prices are shown on the website and can change at any time. You pay the price shown when you order. Paperback delivery is charged extra.
  • Offers, combos and promo codes are valid only for the dates and number of people stated, and we may end them early.
  • The booklets follow the NCERT and CBSE syllabus to help students study. They are not an NCERT publication, and we cannot promise any exam result.
  • We may update these policies. The latest version is always on this page.

2Online book and account rules

  • One ID, one device. Your login ID works on one phone or tablet only. It locks to the first device you log in on.
  • Device reset. If you change your phone, we can move your ID to the new one. 2 device resets a year are free; more are given only in exceptional cases, at our discretion.
  • Validity. A bought online book is valid for 1 year (365 days) from the day you get your login. After that, the chapters lock until you renew.
  • Monthly plan. If you choose the monthly plan, each payment opens the chapters for the period shown (30 days unless stated). The chapters lock when the paid period ends and open again as soon as you renew. You can stop any time by not renewing.
  • Don't share your ID or the pages. Every page shows your name and number. If an ID is shared, or pages are copied, screenshotted or forwarded, we may block the ID without a refund.
  • Promo codes give free access for a limited time to the people they are meant for. A promo book opens only on the phone and browser where the code was entered.
  • Your reading record (pages read, time spent, last read) is shown to you and to Adeel Sir, to help you study. See Privacy below.

3Refund and cancellation

Online book (bought)

  • You can ask for a full refund within 3 days of receiving your login ID, only if you have not logged in even once.
  • After the first login, the online book cannot be refunded.

Monthly plan

  • The payment for the current month (or paid period) is not refunded.
  • You can stop any time: just don't renew. Nothing more is charged.

Paperback

  • You can cancel a paperback order any time before it is dispatched, for a full refund. Once dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.
  • If the book arrives damaged or wrong, tell us on WhatsApp within 5 days of delivery, with photos or an unboxing video.
  • Send back the damaged or wrong book as we guide you. After we receive it, we send you a new copy. Damaged or wrong books are replaced, not refunded.

Free and promo access

  • Free samples and promo-code access are free, so there is nothing to refund.

To ask for a refund or replacement, message us on WhatsApp or email (see Contact) with your name, phone number and order details. An approved refund is paid back to the same UPI or bank account you paid from.

4Shipping and delivery (paperback)

  • We deliver all over India.
  • Orders are dispatched within 3–5 working days after payment.
  • The delivery charge depends on your location. We tell you the exact amount on WhatsApp before you pay.
  • We share the courier tracking details on WhatsApp after dispatch. Delivery time after dispatch depends on the courier and your area.
  • Please give a complete address and a working phone number. If a parcel comes back because of a wrong address or no one to receive it, sending it again is charged.

5Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Your name, WhatsApp number and, for the paperback, your delivery address (shared with us on WhatsApp).
  • For the online reader: your login ID, the type of device and browser you log in on (for example "Android · Chrome"), logins tried from other devices, and your reading record (pages read, time spent, last read).
  • For a promo code: the name and number you enter.

Why we use it

  • To give you access to your chapters, keep your ID to one device, stop sharing, help you on WhatsApp, deliver your paperback, and show you and Adeel Sir your reading progress.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone except as needed to run the service (for example the courier for delivery).

Where it is kept

  • Account and reading data are stored in Google Firebase. Your browser also keeps your last page and settings on your own device.
  • We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to see, correct or delete it at any time; deleting it ends your online access.

Students under 18

  • Students under 18 should order and use the online book with a parent's or guardian's consent.

We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. For any privacy question or complaint, email us (see Contact).
